Welcome to The Leaf Confederacy! This is a community where all leaf lovers(and people who just want to be part of a fun group) can come together and share their exciting ideas! To join, all you need to do is sign your name on this form. The goal of this community is to create a community that will not only work together but to create public web pages anyone in the community can access. This is a supportive community so please be nice to everyone and do not mess with public documents. Apart from that, have fun!
