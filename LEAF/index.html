Welcome to The Leaf Confederacy!

This is a community where all leaf lovers (and people who just want to be part of a fun group) can come together and share their exciting ideas!

The goal of this community is to create a group that will not only work together but create public web pages anyone in the community can access. This is a supportive space so please be nice to everyone and do not mess with public documents. Apart from that, have fun!

Sign Up & Agreement

By signing this form, you will be agreeing to the following terms. Anyone who breaks these terms is subject to removal from the community:

  • You will respect all community google docs and not vandalise or make any major changes without permission from an owner.
  • You will respect the choices and creations of all members in the community.
  • You will unconditionally work with others in the Leaf Community whenever possible.
  • You will not join the Union of Kaeden. They are our sworn enemies and we will fight them.

By accepting these conditions, you can access all of the creations of the Leaf Confederacy and can edit public documents. Please type your name and only your name into the box below if you want to become a part of the Leaf Community.
